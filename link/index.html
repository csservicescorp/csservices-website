How we work · The Siman Method

A process you can follow.

The Siman Method is how we organize information, communication and follow-up on every job. You always know what was requested, what was agreed and what comes next.

  1. DiagnoseWe learn what you need, the property, how urgent it is and how we get access.
  2. DocumentThe request, photos, conditions found, scope and proposal are gathered in one place.
  3. RespondWe coordinate the visit, the approvals and the next step with the right people.
  4. ReportFindings, actions taken, recommendations and updates are recorded as the work moves.
  5. ReviewWe go over the recorded result, any decisions you need to make and anything still open.
  6. OptimizeYour property's history guides the next request and future maintenance.

The method was developed by Álvaro Siman from day-to-day field work on South Florida properties. How much is documented depends on the size and type of each job. More about the method

What you can follow

Your property’s history stays connected.

Each job is kept as a service record. Owners, boards and managers can see what was asked, what was approved and what was done, and the next visit starts from that history instead of from zero.

Service recordWhat it holds
Request & scope
What was reported, where on the property, and the agreed scope of work.
Proposals & approvals
The proposal sent and who approved it, so decisions are easy to trace.
Photos & field notes
Photos of the conditions found and of the work, with notes from the crew.
Updates & open items
Progress updates and anything still pending or waiting on a decision.
Property history
Past jobs on the same property, ready to support the next request or maintenance plan.

This shows how records are organized. It is not a live view, and the contents of each record depend on the job.
