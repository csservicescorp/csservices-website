Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers about parking lot sealcoating and striping for HOAs, condominiums, and commercial properties.

Do residents have to move every car at once?

No. We plan the work in sections and coordinate with management, so residents and visitors always have somewhere to park while each section is sealed and striped.

How long before cars can park on a sealcoated area?

Usually the next day, depending on temperature and humidity. We confirm the surface is ready before reopening each section.

Do you repaint handicap spaces, guest spaces, and fire lanes?

Yes. We repaint parking stall lines, accessible space symbols and access aisles, guest and reserved stencils, no parking zones, arrows, wheel stops, and curbs, following the existing layout or a new one approved by the board.

How often should a parking lot be sealcoated?

In South Florida sun and rain, most asphalt lots benefit from sealcoating every two to three years, with striping refreshed whenever lines start to fade.
